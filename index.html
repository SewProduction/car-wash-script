FAQ

Questions before you download

The quick answers to what people ask most about the Wash the Car Script.

Is the Wash the Car Script free to use?
Yes. The script is free and open source. You can download it from GitHub, inspect the code, and modify the wash stages, particle triggers, and payout hooks to fit your own project.
Which Windows versions are supported?
The script runs on Windows 10 and Windows 11. Any standard modern setup works — just download the latest release and follow the drop-in setup steps.
How do the soap, wax, and particle triggers work?
Each wash stage runs on its own timer. Water and foam particle triggers fire based on vehicle position and stage progress, while the soap and wax phases hand off automatically so coverage stays consistent from rinse to finish.
What happens when the bucket runs low?
The bucket refill logic monitors capacity during the loop and tops it up automatically when levels drop. That keeps the auto-wash loop running without pauses, and cash payout hooks still fire on every completed wash.
